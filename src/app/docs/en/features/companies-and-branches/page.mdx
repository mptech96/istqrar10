# Companies & Branches Management

The multi-tenant architecture organizes inspection entities into hierarchical companies and localized branches.

---

## Structure

```
Company (e.g. Al-Amal Retail Group)
 ├── Commercial Registration (CR) & Tax Details
 ├── Assigned Company Admins
 └── Branches
      ├── Riyadh Main Branch (Branch Manager, Geolocation)
      ├── Jeddah North Branch (Branch Manager, Geolocation)
      └── Dammam Distribution Center
```

---

## Key Capabilities

### Company Administration
- **Company Profile**: Legal business names in Arabic & English, registration numbers, subscription tier.
- **Inspectors Assignment**: Allocates licensed auditors to specific company domains.
- **Aggregated Analytics**: Tracks safety & quality scores across all operational units.

### Branch Management
- **Geolocation & Mapping**: Integrated with `@vis.gl/react-google-maps` and `maplibre-gl` for pinpoint branch navigation.
- **Branch Dashboard**: Specific view for branch managers showing upcoming scheduled visits and open corrective tickets.
