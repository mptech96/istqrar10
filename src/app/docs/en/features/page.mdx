# Features & Modules Overview

Istqrar is divided into specialized modules tailored to each operational role in the auditing lifecycle.

---

## Module Index

### [Visits & Audits](/docs/features/visits)
The central workflow engine of the platform. Covers visit scheduling, dynamic questionnaire execution, evidence uploads, AI-driven observations, and automated report generation.

### [Companies & Branches](/docs/features/companies-and-branches)
Multi-tenant organizational structure allowing companies to register branches, assign physical locations, and delegate inspectors.

### [Checklists & Criteria](/docs/features/checklists-and-criteria)
Flexible compliance framework for structuring audits with sections, scoring criteria, and conditional branching rules.

### [Tickets & Issues](/docs/features/tickets)
Corrective action tracking system generated from visit non-compliance items, with assignment, severity levels, and resolution status.
