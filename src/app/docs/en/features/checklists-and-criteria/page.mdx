# Checklists & Criteria

Checklists define the standardization criteria against which inspections are measured.

---

## Checklist Data Hierarchy

```
Checklist Template
 ├── General Metadata (Target Sector, Minimum Passing Score)
 └── Criteria Sections
      ├── Fire & Life Safety
      │    ├── Criteria 1: Fire extinguishers inspected within 6 months
      │    │    └── Condition: Must be mounted between 1.0m - 1.5m
      │    └── Criteria 2: Emergency exits illuminated & unblocked
      └── Food Hygiene & Storage
           ├── Criteria 1: Cold storage temperature <= 4°C
           └── Criteria 2: Food handlers possess valid health certificates
```

---

## Criteria & Conditions Engine

- **Weighting & Scoring**: Each criterion can be assigned a numerical weight contributing to section and overall visit scores.
- **Conditional Logic**: Dynamic display rules show or hide sub-criteria based on parent answers (e.g., if "Deep fryer present" is YES, trigger oil temperature verification).
- **Mandatory Requirements**: Flags non-negotiable items that cause automatic visit failure regardless of score if violated.
